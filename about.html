Our story

Crafting Excellence in Dallas Since 2005

To provide Dallas homeowners and businesses with exceptional custom woodwork that combines beauty, function, and longevity.

Wood Furniture YP started as a small workshop with a passion for woodworking. Our founder, a master carpenter with over 20 years of experience, set out to create furniture that was both artistic and durable. Over the years, we've grown into a full-service carpentry studio serving clients across Dallas.

Today, we remain committed to the same principles: meticulous attention to detail, use of the finest materials, and a dedication to customer satisfaction. Every project, whether a tiny repair or a complete home renovation, receives the same level of care and expertise.
