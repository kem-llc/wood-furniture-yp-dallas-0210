Our story

Dallas's Premier Custom Carpenter

To provide exceptional custom woodwork that enhances homes and lives, using traditional craftsmanship and modern techniques.

Wood Furniture YP was founded in 2010 by master carpenter John P. with a passion for creating beautiful, functional woodwork. What started as a small workshop has grown into a full-service carpentry business serving homeowners throughout Dallas. Our team combines decades of experience with a commitment to quality that shows in every project.

We believe that every piece of wood has potential, and every home deserves craftsmanship that stands the test of time. From custom furniture to kitchen remodels, we approach each project with the same dedication: listening to our clients, using the finest materials, and delivering results that exceed expectations.
