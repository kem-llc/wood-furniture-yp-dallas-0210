Our story

Your Dallas Carpenter for Life

To create beautiful, functional woodwork that enhances your home and stands the test of time.

Wood Furniture YP was founded on a passion for woodworking and a commitment to quality. Starting as a one-man shop in a small garage, we have grown into a trusted name in Dallas carpentry. Every project, big or small, receives the same dedication to craftsmanship and customer satisfaction.

Today, we serve homeowners throughout the Dallas metro area, from custom furniture to outdoor structures. Our team of skilled carpenters combines traditional techniques with modern tools to deliver exceptional results. We believe in building relationships as sturdy as our furniture.
